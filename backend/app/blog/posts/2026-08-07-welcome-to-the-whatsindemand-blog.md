When a company opens a role, every line in that job post is a decision someone had to fight for and defend. Nobody adds a requirement for fun. So when a skill starts showing up again and again across thousands of postings, that's not a trend — that's money moving.

Surveys tell you what people *say* matters. Postings tell you what companies are *paying* for this week. One of those is worth your time. The other is noise.
